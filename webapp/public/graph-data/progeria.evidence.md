## Research gaps

- **Risedronic acid** → FDPS — untested, plausibility 2/3, evidence database, 0 papers on it in the disease, 0 trials
  - edge: Risedronic acid targets FDPS
  - rationale: Risedronate is a clinically available nitrogen-containing bisphosphonate that can inhibit FDPS, the same upstream branch of progerin farnesylation implicated in progeria.
  - caveat: Its pediatric skeletal toxicity, growth-related effects, dosing, and ability to achieve meaningful FDPS inhibition in affected tissues remain major uncertainties, and the cited cellular precedent is pamidronate rather than risedronate.
  - experiment: Test risedronate in LMNA-progerin patient fibroblasts and matched control cells for farnesylation, abnormal nuclear morphology, and senescence before considering any animal study.
  - evidence: "Open Targets / ChEMBL: RISEDRONATE SODIUM acts on FDPS (max clinical stage: phase 4)" (OT:target:FDPS, ChEMBL:CHEMBL1654)
  - mechanism evidence: "our results support that ruxolitinib, by inhibiting FDPS expression that is upstream of FT, impacts the same pathway than FTI. They may then cooperate to revert progerin effects." (PMID:32196928, P14)
