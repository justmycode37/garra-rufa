# tuberous sclerosis

A rare neurocutaneous disorder characterized by multisystem hamartomas, most commonly involving the skin, brain, kidneys, lungs, eye, and heart, and associated with neuropsychiatric disorders.

- **Inheritance:** Autosomal dominant inheritance
- **Gene(s):** TSC1, TSC2, IFNG
- **Also known as:** Bourneville disease; Bourneville syndrome; Bourneville's disease; Bourneville's syndrome; epiloia; TSC; tuberous sclerosis complex; tuberous sclerosis syndrome
