# Alport syndrome

A rare renal disease characterized by glomerular nephropathy with hematuria progressing to end-stage renal disease (ESRD), frequently associated with sensorineural deafness, and occasionally with ocular anomalies.

- **Inheritance:** X-linked dominant inheritance, X-linked inheritance, Autosomal dominant inheritance, Autosomal recessive inheritance
- **Gene(s):** COL4A3, COL4A5, COL4A4
- **Also known as:** Alport deafness-nephropathy; Alport's syndrome; Hereditary Nephritis; Alport\
