## Research gaps

- **AZD8055** → NF1 — barely_tested, plausibility 3/3, evidence in_vitro, 2 papers on it in the disease, 0 trials
  - edge: AZD8055 tested_in malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumor
  - rationale: AZD8055 inhibits the AKT/mTOR arm of RAS signaling, which is directly activated after NF1 loss, and the quoted activity was demonstrated in NF1-null malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumor cells.
  - caveat: The evidence is limited to three in vitro MPNST lines, AZD8055 is not an approved NF1 therapy, and systemic mTOR inhibition can cause clinically important adverse effects.
  - experiment: Test AZD8055 dose responsively in patient-derived NF1 neurofibroma and PNST organoids with matched NF1-knockout and rescue controls.
  - evidence: "The three NF1-null MPNST-derived cell lines were sensitive to AZD8055 with IC50AZD8055 values of 107±30nM, 137±36nM and 67±10nM for 90-8, 88-14 and 96-2 cell lines, respectively." (PMID:26840085, P20)
  - mechanism evidence: "Neurofibromatosis type 1 (NF1) is a common autosomal dominant disorder with an incidence of 1:2,7001 caused by a germline pathogenic variant in the NF1 tumor suppressor gene." (PMID:33507822, P6)
