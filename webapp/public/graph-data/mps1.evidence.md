  - edge: DNA aptamer targets α-l-iduronidase
  - rationale: An aptamer that binds α-L-iduronidase could conceivably stabilize or modify the deficient enzyme central to MPS I, but the quoted evidence demonstrates chemical conjugation rather than functional rescue.
  - caveat: The required effect direction is unspecified, and an aptamer may not enter cells or lysosomes, achieve tissue distribution, or improve enzyme trafficking and clearance.
  - experiment: First screen aptamer variants for increased IDUA activity, lysosomal localization, and reduced dermatan- and heparan-sulfate accumulation in patient fibroblasts.
