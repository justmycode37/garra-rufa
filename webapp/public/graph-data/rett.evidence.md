## Research gaps

- **nicotinamide riboside** → mitochondrial dysfunction — untested, plausibility 2/3, evidence animal, 0 papers on it in the disease, 0 trials
  - edge: nicotinamide riboside rescues mitochondrial dysfunction
  - rationale: Nicotinamide riboside is a clinically usable NAD precursor with broad mitochondrial support, making it a reasonable candidate if mitochondrial dysfunction contributes to Rett progression.
  - caveat: The Rett evidence does not establish mitochondrial dysfunction as primary, and benefit may depend on demonstrating NAD depletion or a responsive biomarker while avoiding adverse effects from excessive NAD-pathway stimulation.
  - experiment: Conduct a randomized, placebo-controlled pilot in children with Rett syndrome using safety, NAD-related metabolic biomarkers, and prespecified mitochondrial measures as outcomes.
  - evidence: "Notwithstanding these limitations, this study serves as a proof-of-concept study showing that NR supplementation can prevent the onset of mitochondrial dysfunction in NEM6." (PMID:42323895, P24)
  - mechanism evidence: "To date, no systematic study of mitochondrial function in individuals with RTT has presented whether these findings represent a primary or secondary effect" (PMID:24453408, P35)
