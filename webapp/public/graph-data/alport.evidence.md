  - edge: combination renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system blockade targets renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system
  - rationale: RAAS blockade is already supported in Alport syndrome, but adding aldosterone blockade to an ACE inhibitor or ARB could counter disease progression or loss of BP control.
  - caveat: Dual renin-angiotensin-aldosterone blockade raises risks of hyperkalemia, acute kidney injury, and hypotension, and genotype-dependent effects on type IV collagen may complicate interpretation.

  - evidence: "The VA NEPHRON-D trial investigated inhibition of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS) hormonal axis on kidney outcomes in a large population of diabetic chronic kidney disease patients." (PMID:37726051, P11)
