# X-linked hypophosphatemia

A rare hereditary renal phosphate-wasting disorder characterized by hypophosphatemia, rickets and/or osteomalacia, and diminished growth.

- **Onset:** Infantile onset
- **Inheritance:** X-linked dominant inheritance
- **Gene(s):** PHEX
- **Also known as:** hereditary hypophosphatemic rickets, X-linked; hypophosphatemic rickets, X-linked; hypophosphatemic rickets, X-linked dominant, X-linked dominant; rickets, vitamin D-resistant; vitamin D-resistant rickets, X-linked; X-linked dominant hypophosphatemic rickets; X-linked hereditary hypophosphatemic rickets; XLH
