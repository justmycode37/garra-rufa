  - edge: phosphate supplementation and alfacalcidol therapy treats autosomal dominant hypophosphatemic rickets
  - rationale: Phosphate with alfacalcidol is mechanistically relevant to XLH and has biochemical and functional support in autosomal dominant hypophosphatemic rickets.
  - caveat: Both conditions have FGF23-mediated phosphate wasting, so the reported benefit may not transfer, and alfacalcidol plus phosphate can cause hypercalcemia or hypercalciuria.
  - experiment: Conduct a small age- and severity-matched trial comparing alfacalcidol plus phosphate with standard supportive care, with phosphate, ALP, calcium, urinary calcium, and growth as primary outcomes.
