## Research gaps

- **TD-139** → Muscle fibrosis — untested, plausibility 3/3, evidence animal, 0 papers on it in the disease, 0 trials
  - edge: TD-139 rescues Muscle fibrosis
  - rationale: TD-139 improved muscle contraction pathways and reduced fibrosis-related gene expression in dystrophin-deficient dyW mice, directly addressing a major DMD mechanism.
  - caveat: The evidence is recent preclinical work and does not yet establish effective exposure, safety, or clinical benefit in humans.
  - experiment: Conduct a first-in-human randomized placebo-controlled dose-escalation study in ambulatory boys with DMD, including DMD MRI and muscle biopsy fibrosis endpoints.
  - evidence: "RNA sequencing of TD-139-treated dyW muscles showed upregulation of muscle contraction pathways and downregulation of fibrosis-related genes." (DOI:10.1101/2025.03.12.642905, P2)
  - mechanism evidence: "The lack of dystrophin leads to muscle fiber degeneration, activation of chronic inflammatory pathways and progressive muscle tissue replacement by fibroblasts and adipocytes, triggering the process leading to fibrosis development" (PMID:34294164, P7)
