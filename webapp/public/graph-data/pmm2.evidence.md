## Research gaps

A mechanism established in the disease and an intervention acting on it that the literature has not (or has) tested in the disease.

- **phosphomannomutase/phosphoglucomutase inhibitors** → phosphomannomutase activity — untested, plausibility 0/3, evidence in_vitro, 0 papers on it in the disease, 0 trials
  - edge: phosphomannomutase/phosphoglucomutase inhibitors targets phosphomannomutase activity
  - rationale: PMM2-CDG is caused by deficient phosphomannomutase activity, whereas phosphomannomutase/phosphoglucomutase inhibitors would be expected to further inhibit the same central pathway rather than correct it.
  - caveat: The cited evidence only identifies PMM/PGM inhibition as a general drug-development strategy and provides no safety, efficacy, or tissue-specific rationale for this multisystem disorder.
  - experiment: Before any therapeutic efficacy study, test candidate inhibitors in patient-derived fibroblasts for PMM2 activity and N-glycosylation to determine whether they worsen rather than rescue the biochemical defect.
  - evidence: "so inhibitors of PMM/PGM may lead to clinically useful compounds." (PMID:11716469, P2)
  - mechanism evidence: "α -cyano-4-hydroxycinnamic acid enhanced PMM2 enzymatic activity in fibroblasts derived from patients and in a nematode model of PMM2-CDG [25]." (PMID:31454904, P8)
