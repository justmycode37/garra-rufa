# Angelman syndrome

A rare genetic neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by moderate to severe intellectual disability, microcephaly, seizures, ataxic gait and distinct abnormal facial shape.

- **Inheritance:** Sporadic, Autosomal dominant inheritance
- **Gene(s):** UBE3A, SNRPN, ATP10A, OCA2
- **Also known as:** Angelman’s syndrome; happy puppet syndrome; puppetlike syndrome
